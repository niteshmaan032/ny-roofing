EPDM Rubber membrane

EPDM Rubber Roofing

One of the most widely used flat roof membranes, with a long track record on homes and commercial buildings. It stays flexible in heat and cold, so it moves with the building instead of cracking, and it covers large roofs efficiently.

TPO Roofing

A white thermoplastic membrane with a heat-reflective surface. It sends sunlight away from the roof, cutting rooftop heat buildup in summer and helping keep the floors below at a steadier temperature.

Warning Signs

Repair or Replace Your Flat Roof?

Small problems caught early can usually be repaired. When the same problems keep coming back, a new roof is often the better investment.

Usually a repairThe roof is sound overall

  • A small leak or one ceiling stain
  • Membrane damage in one area
  • Cracks or seam separation
  • Worn flashing at a parapet or vent
  • Water pooling after rain

Time to replaceRepairs no longer hold

  • Leaks that keep coming back
  • Material breaking down across the roof
  • The same areas patched again and again
  • Wet or failing insulation
  • An aging or badly damaged system

Common Leak Sources

Where Brooklyn Flat Roofs Usually Leak

Most leaks start where two parts of the building meet. We trace the water to its source instead of patching the spot where it shows.

  1. Parapet wall transitions

    Where the roof meets brick, aging flashing and masonry movement open gaps.

  2. Roof drains and scuppers

    Drainage problems keep water sitting on the roof until it finds a way in.

  3. Membrane seams and joints

    Seams crack and separate with age and the seasons’ expansion and contraction.

  4. Skylight perimeters

    The edge where a skylight meets the roof is a frequent entry point.

  5. Chimney flashing

    Worn flashing around a chimney lets water run down inside the wall.

  6. HVAC and vent penetrations

    Rooftop equipment and vents add openings that each need sound flashing.

Why the stain isn’t under the leak

Water moves through insulation, framing, and wall cavities before it shows. The stain can be several feet from where it gets in.

Signs water is traveling

  • Ceiling stains far from outside walls
  • Moisture that appears after heavy rain
  • Water spots that keep growing
  • Musty odors on the upper floors
  • Leaks that return to the same spot

FAQs

Flat Roofing FAQs

Common questions Brooklyn property owners ask before a flat roof repair or replacement.

01

What is the best material for flat roofing in NYC?

EPDM rubber and TPO are the most common choices in NYC. Both are durable, flexible, and stand up well to the weather. The right one depends on your building, its drainage, and how the roof is used.

02

Why does water pool on my flat roof?

Ponding usually comes from poor drainage, an improper installation, or a structural issue. Regular inspections catch these problems early, before the standing water damages the membrane.

03

Can a flat roof be repaired without replacing it?

Yes. Small leaks and localized membrane damage can often be repaired. Extensive damage may call for a full replacement, and we will tell you which one your roof needs.

04

How can I extend the life of my flat roof?

Regular maintenance, timely repairs, good drainage, and an annual inspection all add years to a flat roof.

05

How long does a flat roof replacement take?

Most replacements are finished within a few days. The exact time depends on the size of the roof, the type of building, and the weather.
