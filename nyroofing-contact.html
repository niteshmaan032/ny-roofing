Before You Call

Common Questions About Getting Started

The four we are asked most often before a first visit. Anything else, just call. There is no charge for a question.

01 Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The inspection, the photographs and the written quote cost nothing, and there is no obligation to book the work.

02 How quickly can you come out?

Most inspections happen within a day or two. Active leaks and storm damage are treated as emergencies and seen the same day where we can.

03 Do you handle permits and paperwork?

We do, including filings with NYC and coordination with co-op or condo boards where a building requires it.

04 Which areas do you cover?

All five boroughs: Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island. Call if you are just outside and we will tell you straight.
