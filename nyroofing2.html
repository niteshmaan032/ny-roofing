FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

We’ve answered some of the most common questions Brooklyn property owners ask about our roofing services and process.

01 What types of roofing services do you provide in Brooklyn?

We offer roof repairs, replacements, installations, and maintenance for flat roofs, shingle roofs, slate roofs, and more. Our solutions are tailored to the unique needs of Brooklyn residential and commercial properties.

02 How do I know if my roof needs to be repaired or replaced?

Signs like leaks, water stains, missing shingles, ponding water, or visible wear are common indicators. Our team provides thorough inspections to help you understand the best solution for your roof.

03 How long does a typical roofing project take?

Project timelines depend on the size and complexity of the roof and the type of service needed. Most residential repairs can be completed in a day, while full replacements may take several days.

04 Are you licensed and insured in Brooklyn, NY?

Yes, NY Roofing is fully licensed and insured to perform roofing work in New York City. We follow all local building codes and safety standards to ensure quality and peace of mind.

05 What roofing materials do you recommend for Brooklyn buildings?

We recommend materials based on your building structure, budget, and long-term goals. Popular options include flat roofing systems, asphalt shingles, clay tiles, and slate roofing.

06 Do you offer warranties on your roofing services?

Yes, we offer manufacturer warranties on materials and workmanship warranties on installation. Warranty details vary by project, and we’ll provide full information before work begins.
