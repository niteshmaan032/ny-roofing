Before You Call

Common Questions About Getting Started

Questions we get asked most before a first visit.

Call (646) 838-0441 Request A Free Estimate
01

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The inspection, the photographs, and the written quote cost nothing, and there is no obligation to book the work.

02

How quickly can you come out?

Most inspections happen within a day or two. Active leaks and storm damage are treated as emergencies and seen the same day.

03

Can you repair it, or does the whole roof have to go?

Often a repair is genuinely enough, and we will say so. The quote prices the repair and the replacement separately, with photographs, so the choice is yours to make.

04

Which areas do you cover?

All five boroughs: Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island.
